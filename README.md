VELVET TIDE — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Play Mara Voss, a getaway driver with one last debt to clear, across Solmar — a 600×600 sun-soaked coastal city of neon avenues, an old quarter, a working harbour, a beach and a lighthouse point. Walk or drive seven original cars (plus police cruisers), steal cars, dodge a 0–5 star wanted level with pursuing police cars, foot officers and roadblocks, run 8 story missions plus a finale harbour heist, fight with fists and a pistol, hunt 10 hidden tide coins, and save your progress. HUD with a live minimap, wanted stars, cash, health/armour, clock and three radio stations; all art, sound and music are original and synthesized in the browser. Runs offline from static files, no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD move/drive, Shift sprint, mouse drag to look, E steal/enter/exit car, F punch, click shoot, Space handbrake, R/1-3 radio, C camera distance, M mute, P pause, S save.
